GW2 DPS Coach - class-neutral base engine update
================================================

The Gear Simulator calculation is now split into explicit layers:

1. Level-80 base attributes
2. Equipment (armor, trinkets and active weapon set)
3. Six-rune static bonuses
4. Infusions
5. Food
6. Static sigil attributes
7. Jade Bot Core vitality
8. Might attributes
9. Utility conversion calculated from the pre-utility total
10. Derived stats (critical chance, critical damage, condition duration,
    boon duration and effective power)

No Thief, Mesmer or other profession traits are applied to these base values.
Profession-specific effects should be implemented after this neutral pipeline.

Two-handed weapons use two one-handed weapon stat blocks from the imported
workbook and skip Weapon 2. Main-hand + off-hand counts each weapon once.
