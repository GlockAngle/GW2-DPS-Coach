Trait page navigation persistence fix

- Trait specialization and major-trait selections are now copied from Streamlit widget keys into a durable session-state snapshot.
- Returning to Traits restores all three specialization lines and selected major traits.
- Gear Simulator, Skill Library, Spider Venom and future rotation pages read the durable selection even while Traits widgets are not rendered.
- Loading a saved trait preset or saved gear build updates both widget state and the durable shared selection.
- This fixes Streamlit removing widget-owned keys when navigating away from the Traits page.
