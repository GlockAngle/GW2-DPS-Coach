GW2 DPS Coach - full Tipcat-style buffs update

Layout:
- Equipment and upgrades are on the left.
- Buffs are in a separate panel on the right.

Implemented PvE boon calculations:
- Alacrity: +25% recharge rate; cooldown multiplier uses 1 / (1 + 0.25 * uptime).
- Fury: +25 percentage points critical chance while active, uptime-weighted.
- Might: +30 Power and +30 Condition Damage per stack at level 80, maximum 25.
- Protection: -33% incoming strike damage while active, uptime-weighted.
- Regeneration: 130 + 0.125 * Healing Power HP/s at level 80, uptime-weighted.
- Resolution: -33% incoming condition damage while active, uptime-weighted.
- Swiftness: +33% movement speed while active, uptime-weighted.
- Vigor: +50% endurance regeneration while active, uptime-weighted.

All selected boons and uptime values are included in saved builds.
