Skill Library classification and live damage update

- Specter Shadow Shroud skills are no longer listed as Staff weapon skills.
- Shadow Shroud land/underwater variants are separated correctly.
- Antiquary artifact skills are categorized as Artifact skills.
- Specialization capitalization is normalized.
- Norn transformation subskills and internal blank records no longer pollute normal weapon filters.
- Added classification, damage-ready, and unresolved coverage counters.
- Added optional current-equipped-weapon filtering.
- Skill strike values now show non-critical, critical, and crit-weighted expected strike damage.
- Damage values are linked live to Gear Simulator Power, Precision, Ferocity, selected weapon-strength roll, enemy armor, and Vulnerability.
- Profession, Shroud, and Artifact skills use the currently selected build weapon strength instead of being treated as Dagger.
- Added a Skill Inspector damage summary and transparent damage audit.
- Conditions remain listed separately until the future skill-event engine models condition applications over time.
