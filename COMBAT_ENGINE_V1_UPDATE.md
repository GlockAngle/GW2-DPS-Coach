# Combat Engine v1

Added a real generic damage foundation: immutable character snapshot, strike and condition events, independent packet calculations, formula audit, and a strict total-prediction gate.

Find it under **Antiquary Benchmark → Rotation Replay → Combat Engine v1**.

This build does not pretend to predict the complete benchmark yet. It calculates individual packets without reading observed damage and keeps the total DPS gate closed until the complete timeline and modifiers are connected.
