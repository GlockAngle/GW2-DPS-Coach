Linked Trait Presets Update

- Gear Simulator build manager now shows a Trait preset selector.
- Trait presets are still created and edited on the Traits page.
- Load traits applies the selected trait preset without changing equipment.
- Saved gear builds now remember the linked trait preset name as well as the trait selection snapshot.
- Loading a saved build restores its linked trait preset and trait selections.
- Active trait preset remains part of current_build.json persistence under %LOCALAPPDATA%\ThiefLab.
