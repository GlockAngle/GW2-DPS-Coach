BUFF PROFILE UPDATE

- Added Full Benchmark Boons and Custom buff profiles.
- Full Benchmark Boons is the default for a fresh setup.
- It applies 25 Might and 100% uptime for Alacrity, Quickness, Fury,
  Protection, Regeneration, Resolution, Swiftness, and Vigor.
- Any manual boon toggle, uptime, or Might-stack change switches the profile to Custom.
- The active buff profile is stored inside saved builds and current_build.json.
- Loading a saved build restores its linked buff profile and boon values.
- The active build manager now shows the linked Trait preset and Buff profile.
