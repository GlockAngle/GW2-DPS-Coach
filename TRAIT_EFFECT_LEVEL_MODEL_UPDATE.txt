TRAIT EFFECT-LEVEL MODEL UPDATE

- Every one of the 108 Thief traits now stores an explicit effects array.
- Each effect has one of three user-facing states:
  * applied_now
  * event_engine
  * needs_implementation
- Mixed traits can now expose passive effects separately from triggered effects.
- Deadly Ambition is explicitly split into +180 Condition Damage (applied now) and Poison on qualifying dual-wield attacks (event engine).
- Trait dropdown/status icons are standardized:
  * green check = all modelled effects applied now
  * yellow = mixed applied + event effects
  * hourglass = waiting for event engine
  * gray = needs implementation
- Trait Progress now reports effect-level counts and offers effect and trait summary views.
- Selected Trait status is also counted per effect.
