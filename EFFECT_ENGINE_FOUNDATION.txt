EFFECT ENGINE FOUNDATION

- Added utils/effect_engine.py as the shared typed effect registry.
- Every active trait effect is now registered with its exact trait source,
  target bucket, value, destination, and condition metadata.
- The registry remains compatible with the existing stat/damage formulas by
  generating the legacy stats/modifiers dictionaries.
- Damage modifier audits now receive individual trait source names instead of
  one combined "Selected traits" source.
- Future runes, sigils, relics, skills, and rotation effects can register into
  the same engine without adding page-specific formulas.
