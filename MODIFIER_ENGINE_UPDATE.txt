Condition modifier engine update

- Sigil of Bursting now automatically adds +5% outgoing condition damage.
- Vulnerability remains a separate incoming damage multiplier.
- With Bursting and 25 Vulnerability, the final condition multiplier is:
  1.05 x 1.25 = 1.3125 (131.25%).
- The Calculated damage modifiers panel now shows source lines and running totals.
- Condition audits use the outgoing modifier before applying Vulnerability.
- Modifier inputs remain read-only and are generated by equipped effects.
